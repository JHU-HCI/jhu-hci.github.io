About the Series

The JHU HCI Seminar — historically known within the community as the HCI Brown Bag — is our recurring forum for invited talks, works-in-progress, paper clinics, faculty AMAs, and community gatherings. It brings together students, postdocs, and faculty from across Hopkins who work on human-computer interaction and human-centered AI.

## Abstract

The promise of academic AI often stalls at the ?last mile? between technical innovation and real-world adoption. In this lecture, Cindy Brummer will share lessons from working with a Malone Center Team on Circlage, where the challenge was not just making advanced AI work, but making it usable, trustworthy, and ready for practice. She will explore how human-centered design, workflow alignment, and iterative collaboration can transform academic research into solutions that clinicians can adopt and organizations can commercialize. The session will highlight practical strategies for bridging the gap between invention and implementation, showing why the last mile of AI is ultimately human.

## Speaker Bio

Cindy Brummer is the Founder and Creative Director of Standard Beagle Studio, a user experience agency in Austin, Texas. She helps B2B SaaS and health tech organizations solve complex problems through UX strategy, research, and product design, with a growing focus on AI and UX. Cindy has worked with a Malone Center Team to develop Circlage, an AI-powered cloud platform to assess surgical skill and offer personalized feedback. Cindy is an accredited UX professional, a mentor to hundreds of designers, and a former UX instructor at the University of Texas at Austin. She also leads the UX in ATX community and is a contributing author to 97 Things Every UX Practitioner Should Know by O?Reilly.

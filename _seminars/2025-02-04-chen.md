## Abstract

Will programming become obsolete? Is AI replacing software engineering? Far from it?demand for developers is growing, but the way we learn and practice programming is rapidly evolving. In this talk, I'll explore how we can rethink programming learning and development in the AI era. I'll present three projects: VizProg, a visualization system that helps instructors identify student misconceptions in real time (CHI 2023 Best Paper Honorable Mention); VizGroup, an AI-assisted system that guides instructors' attention to critical issues in group discussions and coding activities (UIST 2024); and Semanticon, a programming system that supports users to easily encode their semantic intent to web automation programs (UIST 2022 Best Paper Honorable Mention). These systems demonstrate how intelligent visualization and interaction techniques can transform programming into a more fluid and collaborative experience. Looking ahead, I envision AI not just supporting help-seeking but enabling new forms of team collaboration and idea generation that were previously unimaginable.

## Speaker Bio

Yan Chen is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Virginia Tech (USA) where he directs the Programming with Intelligent Machines & Environments Lab (PRIME). His research spans HCI, developer support tools, and programming learning at scale. Yan was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Toronto (Canada). He received his Ph.D. degree from the University of Michigan (USA). His work has been published at top HCI conferences, including ACM CHI, UIST, and CSCW. He received the Best Short Paper award at VL/HCC 2020, the Best Paper at L at S 2024, and the Best Paper Honorable Mention Award at CHI 2023, and UIST 2022.

## Abstract

Unstructured data is everywhere in our daily lives, including meeting and interview transcripts, written notes, and social media data. These data often contain valuable knowledge and insights. To extract such insights, researchers have traditionally relied on qualitative analysis?a widely used method across disciplines such as social science, psychology, and human-computer interaction. However, this approach typically involves multiple manual steps, including open coding, grouping codes, and identifying themes, which are time-consuming and labor-intensive, thereby limiting scalability and generalizability. In this talk, Jie Gao will introduce several innovative methods and tools that she has designed and developed over the past few years to enhance qualitative analysis with the help of AI. She will also discuss how to reflect on these methods from a theoretical perspective, as well as their potential applications and evaluation approaches.

## Speaker Bio

Jie Gao is a Malone Postdoctoral Fellow (since March 2025) in the School of Computer Science and the Malone Center for Engineering in Healthcare, working with Mark Dredze, Ziang Xiao, and Chien-Ming Huang. Previously, she was a postdoctoral researcher with the Mens, Manus, and Machina (M3S) team at the Singapore-MIT Alliance for Research and Technology, where she worked with Thomas W. Malone. She earned her PhD in Information Systems Technology and Design from the Singapore University of Technology and Design in January 2024, under the supervision of Simon Perrault. Her research interests lie in Human-AI Collaboration and AI for Social Science. More specifically, she focuses on building, theorizing, and evaluating innovative AI methods and tools from a human-centered perspective, particularly in the context of qualitative data analysis.
